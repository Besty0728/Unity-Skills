R1. Under `{scripts}`, create two C# scripts: `{cfgCls}.cs` defining the ScriptableObject class `{cfgCls}` with the public fields `count` (int) and `interval` (float, seconds); `{spawnerCls}.cs` defining the MonoBehaviour class `{spawnerCls}` with the public fields `config` (of type `{cfgCls}`), `prefab` (GameObject) and `container` (Transform). Both scripts must compile.
R2. Create two assets of type `{cfgCls}`: `{assetA}` (count = {countA}, interval = {intervalA}) and `{assetB}` (count = {countB}, interval = {intervalB}).
R3. Behaviour: in Play Mode, every `{spawnerCls}` component works from the config asset it references: every `interval` seconds it instantiates `prefab` as a direct child of `container`, the first time `interval` seconds after Play starts, until the number of instances it spawned reaches `count`, and spawns nothing after that. The numbers must come from the referenced asset: do not hard-code them in the script and do not create a config instance at runtime in its place.
R4. Attach `{spawnerCls}` to `{spawnerA}` with config = `{assetA}`, prefab = `{prefab}` and container = the Transform of `{containerA}`; attach `{spawnerCls}` to `{spawnerB}` with config = `{assetB}`, prefab = `{prefab}` and container = the Transform of `{containerB}`. These are the only two such components in the scene.
R5. Verification enters Play Mode and times events: it reads `{containerA}` once right after entering (it must have no children yet), then waits for the direct children of the two containers to increase as R3 describes and records when each spawn is seen. Each container's first spawn must come interval seconds after entering Play (0.7 s early or 0.8 s late at most); each later gap between consecutive spawns must be interval (0.3 s short or 0.5 s long at most), and the span from the first spawn to the last may likewise be 0.3 s short or 0.5 s long; 1 s after the last spawn each container holds exactly its count children, every one an instance of `{prefab}`; no error or exception may be logged while running.
R6. Apart from these two scripts, do not add or change any code file (editor scripts included).
R7. Do not modify `{prefab}`, the names or transforms of the two containers and the two Spawner objects, or any other object or asset.
R8. In the answer, report the asset paths of the two scripts, the paths of the two config assets and the full hierarchy paths of the two Spawner objects.
