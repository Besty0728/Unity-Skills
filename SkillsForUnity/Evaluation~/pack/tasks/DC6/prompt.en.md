R1. Under `{scripts}`, create two C# scripts, each declaring one MonoBehaviour class in the global namespace named like its file:
   - `{mgr}.cs` with `public class {mgr} : MonoBehaviour`, a public field `int score` that starts at 0, and a method `public void Add(int amount)` that adds `amount` to `score`;
   - `{coin}.cs` with `public class {coin} : MonoBehaviour` and the public fields `int value` and `{mgr} manager`.
   The project must compile without errors.
R2. Behaviour of `{coin}` in Play Mode: when a collider whose GameObject is tagged `Player` enters the coin's trigger, the coin calls `manager.Add(value)` and then deactivates its own GameObject with `gameObject.SetActive(false)` (do not destroy it). A coin adds its value at most once in total, no matter how many `Player` colliders touch it or when.
R3. Attach one `{mgr}` to `{board}`; it must be the only `{mgr}` in the scene. Attach exactly one `{coin}` to each of the 7 coin objects directly under `{coins}` and to no other object, and set in the scene for every coin: `manager` = the `{mgr}` component on `{board}`, and `value` = {gold} if the coin's name starts with `Gold` or {silver} if it starts with `Silver`.
R4. Physics of the coins: make the existing `SphereCollider` of every coin a trigger (`isTrigger = true`) without giving any coin another collider, and put every coin object on layer `{loot}` (layer {iloot}). In this project's layer collision matrix, `{actor}` (layer {iactor}, the layer of `{player}` and `{box}`) collides with `{loot}` but not with `Default`.
R5. `{player}` and its child `{box}` are both tagged `Player`; when Play Mode starts, the `{mover}` component on `{player}` moves them along the waypoints under `{route}`, and nothing else may move them. Do not change the `{mover}` component, `{route}` or its waypoints, or the names, tags, layers, transforms, active state or colliders of `{player}` and `{box}`, and do not add colliders to them; adding components that are not colliders is allowed.
R6. Do not change the layer collision matrix, the layer names, the tags or any other project setting.
R7. Do not add, delete or modify any code file other than the two scripts of R1 (the `{mover}` script and editor scripts included), and do not modify other objects: `{board}`, `{coins}` and the coins keep their names, hierarchy paths, positions, rotations, scales and active state.
R8. Verification enters Play Mode, waits until `{player}` has reached its last waypoint, then reads `score` on `{board}` and the active state of every coin: `score` must equal the sum of the values of exactly those coins whose sphere was touched by a collider of `{player}` or `{box}` somewhere along the path, each counted once; exactly those coins must be inactive and every other coin active; no error or exception may be logged while running.
R9. In the answer, report the asset paths of the two scripts, the full hierarchy path of `{board}`, and `finalScore`: the value `score` will have once `{player}` has finished its path.
